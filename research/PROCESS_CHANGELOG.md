- 2026-10-20: Process refinement — an explicit **evidence tier** was added for sources that are neither peer-reviewed studies nor practitioner anecdotes, and a **falsification-handling rule** was adopted. (i) Evidence tier: public, dated, preregistered research ledgers (a hypothesis document that fixes the signal, the data source, the thresholds and the pass bars before the test runs, and publishes the verdict and the trial count) are classified as a distinct tier between peer-reviewed evidence and practitioner material. They are ranked above practitioner sources because the prediction, the sample and the thresholds are auditable and the verdict is dated and irreversible; they are ranked below peer-reviewed work because they are unreviewed, unreplicated, self-published, and their own authors disclaim profitability. Every catalogue entry citing such a source must state the sample, the window and the non-peer-reviewed status, and must not describe it as a replication. (ii) Falsification-handling rule: when a hypothesis's *own registered prediction* has been directly tested and failed, the entry is downgraded or rejected on the evidence dimension and is not re-specified to dodge the result — the CRYPTO-EDGE-001 case shows the permitted move (restructuring the prediction into a narrower, better-identified claim that the failed test did not address, with the failed claim explicitly withdrawn and the new claim given its own falsification criteria) and the forbidden move (re-labelling the same return claim with a different threshold, window or venue). The rule exists because the most likely way a hypothesis catalogue rots is a sequence of well-meant re-specifications after each negative result, which converts a research queue into a list of unfalsifiable stories. (iii) A standing prior is recorded for the next cycles: in crypto, crowding-style signals have now been contradicted in three separate preregistered tests, all pointing the same way — they accompany continuation rather than exhaustion — so any new contrarian candidate in this domain must be argued against that prior before it can be promoted.
