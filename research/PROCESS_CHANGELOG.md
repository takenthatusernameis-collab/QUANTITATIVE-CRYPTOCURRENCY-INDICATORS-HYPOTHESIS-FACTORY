- 2026-10-20: Scoring-formula correction applied across the catalogue. The published rubric defines each dimension's contribution as (score / 5) x dimension weight, summing to 100. The cycle-1 records entered the two 10%-weight dimensions (falsifiability, originality) at half their proper value, so CRYPTO-EDGE-001 was recorded as 67 (correct 73) and CRYPTO-EDGE-002 as 53 (correct 58 at cycle-1 dimension scores, 61). Reason: the rubric is the single source of ranking truth and it must be applied identically to every dimension and every entry; silently leaving it would propagate an arithmetic error into every future comparison and would make rank changes look like evidence changes when they are not. Expected benefit: ranking now reflects dimension scores only, so future movement in a total is attributable to evidence or judgement rather than to formula drift. All totals and the shortlist were recomputed; no dimension score was changed except the one documented robustness downgrade on CRYPTO-EDGE-002.
- 2026-10-20: Fourth research cycle completed. Corrected a substantive evidence characterisation error: the sample of the published carry-crash research (BIS WP 1087 revised October 2025; Management Science May 2026) runs March 2019 to July 2024, not to January 2022 as cycles 1–3 recorded, so the untested window moves to after July 2024 and to the transfer from dated futures to retail perps. Added three independent contrary-evidence sources (momentum explanation of the futures–spot gap; perp-era dislocation compression; event-heterogeneous, out-of-sample-inverting crash precursors) and added CRYPTO-EDGE-003 (cross-venue funding dispersion) with two supporting primary sources. One robustness downgrade (CRYPTO-EDGE-002), one rejection set and two deferrals recorded in REJECTED_AND_DEFERRED.md. Totals: CRYPTO-EDGE-001 73, CRYPTO-EDGE-003 61, CRYPTO-EDGE-002 58. No empirical validation performed.
- 2026-10-20: Process refinement — every catalogue entry now records, in the evidence notes, which claims are published findings, which are mechanistic reasoning, and which are unverified assumptions, together with the access classification and date checked for each source; and each hypothesis's validation plan now names a mandatory baseline that represents the strongest competing explanation (momentum-matched entry for CRYPTO-EDGE-001/002, liquidity-filter and sign-placebo controls for CRYPTO-EDGE-003). Reason: the two existing entries failed to distinguish "the signal is an imbalance proxy" from "the signal is a momentum proxy", which is the most likely way a backtest could produce a false positive. Expected benefit: a validator can no longer obtain a positive result for a derivatives-positioning signal that is only a repackaged momentum rule.
