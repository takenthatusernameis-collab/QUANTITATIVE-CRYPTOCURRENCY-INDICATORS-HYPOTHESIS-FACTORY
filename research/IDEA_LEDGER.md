# IDEA LEDGER

This is the complete catalogue of research-only systematic cryptocurrency edge hypotheses. No empirical validation is performed in this repository.

Stable IDs: CRYPTO-EDGE-001, CRYPTO-EDGE-002.

## CRYPTO-EDGE-001 — Basis/Carry Reversal (Cash-and-Carry Crash Predictor), v1.0

1. Short name and version: Basis/Carry Reversal v1.0.
2. One-sentence thesis: Sustained elevated perpetual-futures basis (carry) relative to spot signals crowded leveraged longs and predicts elevated sell-liquidation risk and negative spot returns over the following weeks, because constrained arbitrage cannot immediately absorb the positioning imbalance.
3. Status: new.
4. Proposed edge family and target market/universe: Derivatives pricing/positioning anomaly (cost-of-carry / constrained arbitrage); BTC and ETH USDT-margined perpetuals versus spot on major retail-accessible venues (Binance, Bybit, OKX); cross-venue where public data permits.
5. Mechanism: When spot demand outpaces futures-arbitrage capacity, perp basis widens positive. Cash-and-carry arbitrage (long spot, short perp) is constrained by absent cross-margining, leverage caps, custody and funding costs, and short availability — per BIS WP 1087, high carry then forecasts future price crashes and sell liquidations rising as a share of open interest. Crowding persists because arbitrage capital is slow, risk-limited, and itself exposed to liquidation if prices move against the carry trade before convergence. Chi (2023) documents the basis as the strongest cross-sectional crypto futures factor, consistent with spot-to-futures information segmentation.
6. Exact falsifiable prediction: When annualized BTC/ETH perp basis exceeds a preregistered positive threshold (theory-derived starting value +20%) for a sustained period, spot returns over the next 1–4 weeks are negative on average and sell liquidations as % of open interest rise; when basis is negative or near zero, no directional prediction is made. Direction: basis high → down. Horizon: 1–4 weeks.
7. Candidate explanatory variables and proposed signal concept: Signal = annualized perp basis (mark price minus spot index) / spot index, annualized using the funding/settlement cadence, computed at each 8-hour funding timestamp from only data known at that close (no look-ahead). Plain language: "is futures expensive relative to spot right now?" Forward window starts after the funding close at which the signal is observed.
8. Expected trade expression: Short perp (or reduce long exposure) when basis is extreme; optionally cash-and-carry unwind (long spot, short perp) where borrow and funding allow. Shorting is treated as a separate feasibility question (borrow cost, exchange rules). Not market-neutral by default; applied only in the basis-extreme regime.
9. Primary comparator or null explanation: Random-entry short with the same holding horizon and fee model; spot buy-and-hold over the same windows; basis computed on a venue-shuffled or lagged series as a placebo. High basis could also simply reflect rational cost of carry in a trending bull market, not crowded positioning.
10. Data required, source URLs, free-access classification, granularity, likely history, coverage, limitations: perpetual mark price, spot index price, open interest, long/short liquidation notionals, funding rates; 8-hourly or daily; history at least 2 years; sources: exchange public market-data documentation/endpoints (Binance, Bybit, OKX), BIS WP 1087 dataset documentation. Access classification: 1 (confirmed free, public) for mark price, spot index, funding on major venues; open interest classification 2 (apparently free, granularity/history limits unverified); liquidation notionals classification 2 (aggregate figures published by some venues; per-venue granularity and limits unverified).
11. Plausible friction and capacity considerations (theoretical, no measured values): funding paid on the short side erodes carry; short availability and borrow costs; slippage entering during stress; exchange leverage caps and liquidation of the arbitrage trade itself before convergence; turnover is limited to basis-extreme regimes so capacity is modest. No friction values are invented.
12. Why meaningfully different from the catalogue and prior art: Grounded in documented basis premium (Chi 2023) and carry-crash predictability (BIS 2023), with a precise falsifiable liquidation-linked prediction. Distinct from plain moving-average crossovers and from CRYPTO-EDGE-002 because it keys off the level of cost of carry, not funding-flow extremes.
13. Strongest argument against; likely failure regimes; confounders: Basis can remain elevated through long trending bull markets (carry may be profitable while the trend continues); crashes may be driven by exogenous news rather than positioning; BIS sample runs only to roughly Jan 2022 and predates current venue structure; liquidation data is noisy and often proxied. Likely failure regimes: strong trending markets, exogenous shock events, regime change in venue leverage rules.
14. Research-only validation plan to be executed elsewhere: preselect the basis threshold; define entry at funding close and exit at subsequent funding closes over 1–4 weeks; baseline versus random entry; theoretical friction cost model (funding, spread, slippage, borrow); out-of-sample split; regime coverage (bull, bear, sideways); falsification criteria: no negative forward return after high basis, or no rise in sell liquidations as % of OI. Do not execute this plan in this repository.
15. Evidence notes with sources and dates: BIS WP 1087 "Crypto carry" (2023-03-24), checked 2026-10-20, confirmed free — high carry predicts spot crashes and sell liquidations (mechanism/literature, not a result from this repo). Chi (2023) J. Futures Markets doi:10.1002/fut.22425, checked 2026-10-20, abstract only — basis strongest cross-sectional factor (full text access unverified). Liu & Tsyvinski (2021) NBER w24877, context only. Label: mechanistic reasoning and published findings separated from unverified assumptions (preregistered threshold +20% is an assumption, not evidence).
16. Scores, total rank, confidence, explanation: Causal mechanism 4/5 × 20% = 16; evidence quality 4/5 × 15% = 12; robustness 3/5 × 15% = 9; free-data accessibility 5/5 × 15% = 15; net value after frictions 3/5 × 15% = 9; falsifiability 4/5 × 10% = 4; originality 2/5 × 10% = 2. Total 67/100. Confidence: medium — mechanism and evidence are solid, but originality is low (cash-and-carry is well documented) and regime dependence limits durability. Rank: high as a baseline/comparator, moderate as a novel edge.
17. Next most valuable research question: whether the carry-crash relation holds post-2022 across current perpetual venues, and whether a preregistered basis threshold survives an out-of-sample period without overfitting.

## CRYPTO-EDGE-002 — Funding Extreme Contrarian (Crowded Leverage Unwind), v1.0

1. Short name and version: Funding Extreme Contrarian v1.0.
2. One-sentence thesis: Extreme funding-rate readings (top/bottom decile of the trailing distribution) signal overcrowded leveraged positioning prone to forced unwind, predicting short-horizon mean reversion opposite to the funding extreme.
3. Status: new.
4. Proposed edge family and target market/universe: Derivatives positioning/flow anomaly (crowded leverage, forced liquidation feedback); BTC and ETH perpetuals on retail-accessible venues with public funding history.
5. Mechanism: Positive funding means longs pay shorts — crowded longs. When sentiment turns, leveraged longs hit maintenance margin; the exchange emits forced, price-insensitive market sells, pushing price further down into the next thresholds — the leverage-manufactured feedback documented in the liquidation-cascade literature (Garcia Seuma 2026; BitMEX study showing ~60X average leverage among the liquidated and substantial daily liquidation %). Crowding persists because momentum chasers enter late and leverage is sticky. Mirror image for extreme negative funding (crowded shorts, short-squeeze exposure).
6. Exact falsifiable prediction: Funding rate in the top decile of the trailing 90-day distribution predicts negative returns over the next 1–7 days; bottom decile predicts positive returns. Direction: opposite to funding sign. Horizon: 1–7 days.
7. Candidate explanatory variables and proposed signal concept: Signal = 8-hour funding rate from public exchange data, expressed as a percentile of the trailing 90-day distribution computed using only past data (no look-ahead); signal observed at a funding timestamp, forward window starts after. Plain language: "are traders paying an extreme premium to hold one side?"
8. Expected trade expression: Contrarian — short when funding is extremely positive, long when extremely negative; shorting feasibility is a separate question (borrow, exchange constraints). Not market-neutral; avoid strong trending news regimes as a filter. Alignment note: when contrarian direction coincides with receiving funding, the carry helps rather than hurts.
9. Primary comparator or null explanation: Random entry with the same hold period; spot buy-and-hold; funding series time-shuffled as placebo; CRYPTO-EDGE-001 (basis level) as an alternative positioning proxy to test whether funding adds incremental information.
10. Data required: 8-hour funding rates, mark price, spot price; open interest as context; history at least 2 years; public exchange market-data endpoints/documentation. Access classification: 1 (confirmed free, public) for funding and price on major venues; open interest classification 2 (granularity/history limits unverified).
11. Plausible friction and capacity considerations: funding receipt is favorable in the contrarian direction when sign-aligned, but adverse when it is not; slippage and whipsaws in strong trends can dominate; exchange-specific funding formula and clip; exchange leverage rules affect how quickly unwinds occur. No measured friction values.
12. Why meaningfully different from the catalogue and prior art: Mechanism is funding-rate flow extremes and forced-unwind feedback, distinct from the carry level in CRYPTO-EDGE-001. Distinct from generic price oscillators (e.g., RSI overbought) because it uses derivatives positioning, not price history alone.
13. Strongest argument against; likely failure regimes; confounders: Extremes can persist for extended trending periods (funding can stay positive for months in a bull run); timing of the unwind is unpredictable; false positives are frequent; little rigorous published evidence ties funding percentiles specifically to forward returns — most sources are practitioner/anecdotal. Likely failure regimes: strong momentum regimes, exogenous news shocks.
14. Research-only validation plan to be executed elsewhere: preselect decile thresholds; entry at funding close once an extreme is confirmed using only past data; hold 1–7 days; baseline versus random entry; friction model includes funding receipt (sign-dependent) and slippage; out-of-sample test; regime coverage; falsification: no mean reversion after extremes, or extremes persisting through the trend. Do not execute here.
15. Evidence notes with sources and dates: Garcia Seuma (2026) arXiv:2607.27070 and arXiv:2608.03616, checked 2026-10-20, confirmed free arXiv — forced-liquidation feedback and cascade mechanics (mechanism literature). arXiv:2102.04591 BitMEX leverage/liquidation study, checked 2026-10-20, confirmed free — substantial forced liquidations, ~60X average leverage among liquidated. Gate.io Wiki funding/OI/liquidation article, checked 2026-10-20, free but exchange-published and marketing-adjacent — treated as anecdotal, not evidence. Label: published findings vs. unverified assumptions (decile thresholds are assumptions).
16. Scores, total rank, confidence, explanation: Causal mechanism 3/5 × 20% = 12; evidence quality 2/5 × 15% = 3; robustness 3/5 × 15% = 9; free-data accessibility 5/5 × 15% = 15; net value after frictions 3/5 × 15% = 9; falsifiability 3/5 × 10% = 3; originality 2/5 × 10% = 2. Total 53/100. Confidence: low-medium — mechanism is plausible and data is free, but evidence is thin and timing is noisy. Rank: lower than CRYPTO-EDGE-001; worth keeping only if it adds incremental predictability beyond basis.
17. Next most valuable research question: whether funding extremes add incremental predictability beyond the basis/carry measure (CRYPTO-EDGE-001), or whether the signal is subsumed by it.
