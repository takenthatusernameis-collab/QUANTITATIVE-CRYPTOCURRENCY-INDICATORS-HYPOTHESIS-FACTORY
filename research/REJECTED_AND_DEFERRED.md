# REJECTED AND DEFERRED

Rationale for rejects, duplicates, and ideas awaiting material missing input.

## Rejected (this cycle or earlier) — do not rediscover without a material new fact

- **Funding-extreme contrarian (the idea formerly catalogued as CRYPTO-EDGE-002). Rejected on 2026-10-20** (sixth cycle), after being carried in the catalogue since cycle 1. Reason: the idea's core prediction was preregistered, dated and tested by an independent party, and failed on both of its preregistered criteria with point estimates of the wrong sign. The test used this catalogue's own signal construction — trailing-90-day percentile rank of daily funding, LOW <= 10th and HIGH >= 90th, fixed before the run — on Binance USDT-margined perpetuals across 8 symbols and 17,910 symbol-days from December 2019 to July 2026, which covers the post-July-2024 window this catalogue had flagged as untested. Result: 7-day LOW-minus-HIGH = -0.95%, 95% moving-block bootstrap CI [-3.20%, +1.39%], sign positive in only 4 of 8 symbols against a required 5; HIGH-funding days were followed by +3.30% versus +2.35% at 7 days and +12.61% versus +4.83% at 30 days. The momentum-flavored variant (HIGH funding read as confirmation) was tested in the same ledger and also failed to clear its bar (+7.78%, CI [-0.77%, +17.58%] at 30 days), so the opposite sign was *not* promoted either. Combined with the absence of any published study tying funding percentiles to forward returns, the momentum explanation of the futures-spot gap (He, Manela, Ross and von Wachter 2026), and funding clustering near zero across venues (Zhivkov 2026), the supporting evidence for the registered prediction is empty. Reinstatement requires one of: an independent preregistered test on a disjoint sample and window with the correct sign and at least five of eight symbols positive; a peer-reviewed study establishing contrarian predictability from funding extremes with a mechanism that is not momentum reversal; or a documented structural change in the funding mechanism that would invalidate the failed test's mapping. The forced-liquidation *mechanism* is not what was rejected, and a volatility-forecasting use of funding is a different hypothesis (see the deferred entry below).
- **Perp-basis level as a contrarian / mean-reversion state variable. Rejected on 2026-10-20.** Reason: this is the single-instrument, cross-venue-nested sibling of the rejected funding-extreme idea, and it failed in the same direction on the same machinery. A preregistered test on Binance USDT perpetuals versus spot (17,899 symbol-days, trailing-90-day percentile of the daily basis, 10th/90th deciles fixed in advance) returned a 7-day LOW-minus-HIGH of -2.61% with a 95% block-bootstrap interval of [-4.88%, -0.12%] — the interval excludes zero on the side opposite the registered prediction — with 2 of 8 symbols positive; high-premium days were followed by +4.20% versus +1.59% at 7 days and +14.8% versus +6.1% at 30 days. The momentum-flavored version was noted in the same source as showing significance but was not the registered claim and was closed. This rejection is what drove CRYPTO-EDGE-001's restructure from a directional return claim to a crash-conditionality claim; it is recorded separately because the idea of fading an elevated perp basis is the single most likely thing to be re-proposed.
- **Cross-venue fiat-versus-offshore premium continuation as a standalone directional book. Rejected on 2026-10-20** (sixth cycle, non-derivatives coverage). Reason: the *information* signal is real and preregistered — the peg-adjusted premium of a regulated USD venue over the offshore USDT venues predicted forward returns in the continuation direction at +3.17% per 7 days, CI [+0.51%, +6.08%], breadth 17 of 20 symbols, on 31,752 symbol-days from 2019-01-01 to 2026-08-27, surviving a trailing-7-day-return trim (+2.47%, CI [+0.13%, +4.74%]) and the removal of the two largest contributors (+2.85%, CI [+0.34%, +5.46%]) — but carrying it into a strategy-grade test with next-open fills, 10bp fee, 1bp half-spread and 25bp participation impact per side was KILLED on all three gates: Sharpe 0.89 against a required 1.0, deflated Sharpe 0.36 against a required 0.95, and Sharpe below the equal-weight buy-and-hold benchmark of 0.94 computed in the same run on the same window and engine; correlation with the incumbent book +0.3929 against a 0.30 bar; 2025 and 2026 both negative. The sample was a bull market with an unconditional 7-day forward return of +0.92%, so a long-only crypto book would have passed a naive profitability bar almost regardless. Do not re-propose the same premium signal as a long-only crypto book. A *delta-neutral* cross-venue version was never tested and is not rejected by this entry, but nothing found in this cycle supports it either.
- **Stablecoin supply growth as a crypto-demand / flow proxy. Rejected on 2026-10-20** (sixth cycle, non-derivatives coverage). Reason: two independent problems. First, endogeneity in the same form as the deferred spot-ETF-flow idea — rising crypto prices create demand for stablecoins, so supply growth responds to returns as well as predicting them. Second, and newly documented, the proxy is contaminated: free DefiLlama data show total stablecoin supply above $310B by mid-2026 versus roughly $124B at end-2023, but practitioner decomposition attributes a growing share of that growth to enterprise and payments infrastructure, cross-border payments, AI-agent payments and yield-bearing stablecoins rather than to crypto trading demand, so the supply series increasingly measures fiat-tokenisation activity rather than crypto trading ammunition. The data itself is confirmed free (access 1, https://stablecoins.llama.fi/stablecoincharts/all and https://defillama.com/stablecoins); it is the *mechanism* that fails. Revisit only if a source establishes a return-residualized stablecoin-supply shock with independent price impact, or if supply can be cleanly split into trading-motive and non-trading-motive components with free data.
- **Stablecoin peg deviation (for example USDT trading at a discount) as a directional crypto return signal. Rejected on 2026-10-20** (sixth cycle, non-derivatives coverage). Reason: the closest preregistered test of a stablecoin-peg signal found it was noise at every horizon tested — +0.15% at 1 day, +0.12% at 7 days and -8.86% at 30 days, with breadth of 13/20, 10/20 and 17/20 — and, in the same test, the information was found in the *asset* dislocation rather than in the peg: the peg-adjusted venue premium signalled while the peg component did not. A directional peg signal therefore fails both its own test and the decomposition test. A peg *deviation* used as a stress or regime filter (rather than a directional signal) is not rejected, but no entry for it is proposed.
- Pure cross-venue funding arbitrage as a *return source* (delta-neutral long the low-funding venue, short the high-funding venue, harvesting the funding differential). Rejected in this form on 2026-10-20. Reason: Zhivkov (2026, Mathematics 14(2):346, open access) reports that of the top cross-venue opportunities only about 40% remain profitable after transaction costs and spread reversals, with forced exits in about 95% of cases, and that funding clusters near zero (IQR 1.20 bps for CEX venues) — the differential is usually smaller than round-trip costs. Note that published evidence now disagrees on this point: Finance Research Letters (2026) reports funding-rate arbitrage between CEX and DEX venues with superior risk-adjusted returns to holding the asset, with low correlation to the buy-and-hold return. The disagreement is unresolved; the directional relative-performance question built on the same dispersion measure survives inside CRYPTO-EDGE-003; only the carry-capture return source is rejected.
- Crypto options implied-volatility slope / skew as a *return* predictor. Rejected on 2026-10-20. Reason: published evidence (Journal of Financial Markets, 2024, "Implied volatility slopes and jumps in bitcoin options market", Deribit tick-by-tick data) finds IV slopes forecast realized volatility on a weekly frequency but "lack the predictive capability for returns"; Hoang (2020, Journal of Futures Markets) likewise finds option IV informative mainly for volatility forecasting rather than returns. A volatility-forecasting hypothesis using Deribit data would be a different idea and is not currently justified by this repository's evidence. Revisit only if a source documents return predictive content for a specific crypto option surface feature.
- Parameter-sweep variants of the existing entries (different trailing windows, decile cut-offs, or basis thresholds under new names). Not counted as new hypotheses; the catalogue keeps one preregistered specification per mechanism to avoid multiple-testing contamination.

- **Cross-sectional state-variable conditioning of the cross-sectional momentum spread, without a survivorship-aware universe. Rejected on 2026-10-20** (seventh cycle). Reason: this is the family of CRYPTO-EDGE-004, and the two closest preregistered tests of it are both killed, one of them with a point estimate leaning the opposite way to the registered prediction. The "dispersion dial" test conditions the top-2-minus-bottom-2 forward 7-day momentum spread on the cross-sectional standard deviation of trailing 30-day returns, on the wide 40-coin daily panel over 64,484 symbol-days, and is KILLED: the spread was monotone *increasing* across dispersion terciles (+0.50%, +1.05%, +1.40%) with a top-minus-bottom difference of +0.90% and a 95% block-bootstrap interval of [-2.66%, +3.69%]. The "breadth dial" sibling conditions the same book on the share of the universe with positive trailing returns and is KILLED on the same pattern (+0.90%, +1.15%, +2.92%; top-minus-bottom +2.02%, CI [-2.10%, +5.74%]). The published source of the family (Zhang and Makgolo 2026) had already warned that its own inference is sensitive to dynamic universe construction. Do not re-propose this family without a survivorship-aware universe AND a materially different state variable; the two killed preregistered tests are the prior art. This rejection does not extend to the survivorship-aware version, which is deferred below for a data reason rather than an evidence reason.
- **Flow-conditioned short-horizon reversal as a retail-scale crypto return source (the third non-derivatives family searched for in the ninth cycle). Rejected on 2026-10-20.** Reason: this family clears the free-data bar cleanly, has the strongest published result located in this cycle, and fails only on net economics. Kitron and Wengrowicz (2026, arXiv:2608.21888, open access, with public replication code and frozen results) show that at 15-minute horizons directional mean reversion is far stronger and more pervasive in crypto than in US equities: under one matched, strictly out-of-sample walk-forward protocol, 90% of 183 Binance pairs carry significant directional reversal against 2.7% of 187 US stocks and ETFs, in every focal coin-year since 2021, with a class-mean AUC gap of +0.031 as designed and +0.011 (95% CI [+0.008, +0.014]) under the most conservative accounting, surviving an artifact battery, an exact permutation null and a frozen six-month holdout. The mechanism is documented and coherent: the reversal concentrates after moves driven by aggressive taker flow and grows with flow intensity, while the order-book depth a move consumes conditions nothing, a pattern consistent with compensated liquidity provision. The inputs are free and point-in-time and were confirmed present in the same Binance public archive this cycle (daily/bookDepth/ from 2023-01-01, daily/aggTrades/ and daily/trades/). The rejection is on cost: the gross edge peaks near 1.3 bp per trade against a 5 bp round-trip cost, which the authors themselves describe as "large enough to detect, too small to clear benchmark spot capture costs". This is the cleanest external demonstration in the catalogue that detection robustness and tradability are separate axes in this asset class, and the second instance here (the first was the cross-venue carry capture) of a robustly detected signal failing only on costs. **Reinstatement condition:** an execution venue and price schedule where the round-trip cost is below roughly 1.3 bp (maker rebates, co-located or fee-tier-advantaged execution at institutional scale), or a variant of the signal whose per-trade gross is an order of magnitude larger than this one. Do not re-propose the plain "bet against the previous candle" version on any retail venue.

## Deferred — awaiting a material missing input

*(Status as of the ninth research cycle, 2026-10-20: the token-dilution family and the conditional funding-extreme split remain deferred; the venue-announced delisting family was added as a new deferral; **CRYPTO-EDGE-004's deferral was lifted** and the entry is back on the active shortlist at rank 3 — its entry below is retained for the audit trail, with the update stated in full immediately beneath it.)*

- **Token dilution and supply-schedule premia (the second non-derivatives family; the "predictable supply change" family). Deferred on 2026-10-20** (seventh cycle) rather than promoted, and the deferral is on a data-access ground, not on evidence. Reason: the published result is strong and the mechanism is clean. Guo (2026, SSRN 6636258) documents, on weekly data for 404 coins from 2020 to 2026, that the fully diluted valuation premium and a 12-week dilution rate predict cross-sectional returns with Newey-West t-statistics of -3.13 and -2.79, that portfolio sorts yield annualized long-short spreads of 25-32%, that aggregate dilution risk carries a positive systematic premium (t = +2.72), and that the premium is absent in the top-five blue-chip tokens and in mature coins but concentrated in the first year of trading - which points the mechanism at *limited investor attention to publicly disclosed vesting schedules* rather than at mechanical price pressure, and makes the effect a cross-sectional mispricing rather than a directional claim. The blocker is the input: the signal requires dated circulating supply, fully diluted valuation and vesting schedules, and this cycle established that the CoinGecko free tier refuses every historical query (HTTP 401, error_code 10012, "Public API users are limited to querying historical data within the past 365 days"), that its keyless /coins/markets endpoint exposes only *current* supply fields, and that CoinLore's free API is current-only with a 404 on its chart endpoint. No free source of historical circulating supply was confirmed. **Ninth-cycle update (2026-10-20): this deferral is unchanged, and the reason is now sharper.** The ninth cycle re-checked every free supply and market-cap route and confirmed that historical circulating supply, historical market capitalisation and market-cap rank remain **not** confirmed free: CoinGecko's own API reference documents that the Demo tier (free, and keyless or with a free key) is restricted to the past 365 days, the paid Basic tier to two years, and only Analyst and above carry the full range, so the restriction is a paid-tier artefact that no free key lifts; CoinPaprika's free tier refuses daily historical tickers before the trailing 365 days with an explicit plan error; CoinLore is current-only; DefiLlama's per-token historical supply and vesting endpoints require a Pro key; and CoinMarketCap's keyless tier is current-only with historical listings paywalled. The ninth cycle did resolve the *delisting* half of the survivorship problem - a dated delisting record for venue-delisted tokens is constructible from the Binance public archive's daily kline files - but that does not touch this family, whose signal needs dated circulating supply, fully diluted valuation and vesting schedules specifically. Note also that a dated delisting record would *sharpen* this family's own bias if it were ever unblocked: token dilution and the risk of a near-term unlock are largest in exactly the young, weak tokens that a venue delists, so a survivorship-aware universe would remove a large part of the family\'s apparent cross-sectional spread. Two adjacent constructs in the same family were read and not promoted: the token-unlock event study (Kim 2026, SSRN 6632838: 46 of 52 Binance unlock events show negative returns within 72 hours, mean -16.97%, replication code public, but the 52-event calendar was hand-collected and no free forward-looking unlock calendar was confirmed) and the locked-supply conviction construct (Khoja 2026, SSRN 7314420: a one-standard-deviation-higher staking share earns +0.7% per month among cheap coins and -3.0% among expensive ones, code and data public, but the required per-token locked-supply share is not confirmed obtainable historically on free sources, and the paper is self-described as part of an AI-authored research collection, which is a reliability flag recorded rather than a reason to dismiss it). **Reinstatement condition:** a free, dated, point-in-time source of historical circulating supply, fully diluted valuation or vesting schedules. The same data question also gates CRYPTO-EDGE-004 below, so one answer resolves both families.
- **CRYPTO-EDGE-004, Dispersion-Contingent Momentum Breakdown. Deferred on 2026-10-20** (seventh cycle), moved off the active shortlist at 45/100, confidence low. Reason: the entry's registered prediction was never tested, so the falsification-handling rule is not engaged and this is not a rejection; what failed is the free-data standard for the entry's essential input, in the regime the entry tests. A point-in-time crypto universe is *partially* constructible on free data - a listing-date and trailing-volume screen from the Binance public archive works, and a preregistered audit of exactly that construction retained 58% of Sharpe (1.47 to 0.86) and 49% of CAGR (+42.91% to +21.06%) for an unchanged cross-sectional momentum book, cleared neither a Sharpe of 1.0 nor a deflated-Sharpe bar of 0.95, and reversed its best year - but the *delisting* half of survivorship is not observable on free data at all, and the market-cap screen that would be the natural substitute is unavailable because the CoinGecko free tier refuses historical queries. High cross-sectional dispersion, the entry's own test regime, is precisely where launches, unlocks and delistings concentrate, so the residual bias binds hardest where the hypothesis is tested. **Reinstatement condition:** a free, dated, point-in-time source of historical circulating supply, market capitalisation or a dated delisting record for tokens that a major venue has delisted. If none is found, the correct outcome is a recorded deferral of the whole cross-sectional-survivorship-aware family rather than a repeated re-specification of it.
- **Update, ninth cycle (2026-10-20): the deferral is LIFTED and the entry is back on the active shortlist at rank 3, 45/100, confidence low.** The reinstatement condition was written with an "or", and its second limb is now satisfied: a dated delisting record for tokens a major venue has delisted **is** constructible on free data. It is not a vendor field; it is derived from the Binance public data archive, which keeps a dated daily kline file for every USD-M perpetual symbol ever listed, including delisted ones, and whose terminal file for a ceased contract carries volume=0 and trade count=0 with a frozen close - so the last trading date is exactly the last file with a non-zero trade count. Verified against two independently announced venue delistings (LUNA, last trade 2022-05-12 against the venue's 2022-05-13 delisting; OMG, last trade before the venue's 2025-01-31 settlement after two postponements) and against one re-listing (CVC, zero-volume through 2024 and trading again in September 2026). Two construction hazards are recorded in SOURCE_NOTES.md: the monthly tree is incomplete for some symbols and must not be used, and a symbol's trading lifetime must be reconstructed as segments because re-listings occur. CRYPTO-EDGE-004 is therefore no longer blocked on data, but it is not promoted either: it is re-scored with free-data accessibility 2/5 to 3/5 (the universe is now buildable, but the published result's market-cap screen still has no free history, so a free replication must substitute listing-age and trailing-volume screens and is a replication rather than an exact replication) and net value after frictions 2/5 to 1/5 (Arefev 2026, SSRN 7404139, documents on a survivorship-aware 832-symbol Binance perpetual universe over 2020-2026 that neither permitted cross-sectional momentum specification yields a net-of-costs spread distinguishable from zero, the gross spread of +0.573% per week being of the same order as 0.40pp per week of realistic costs, and the alternative specification flipping sign with an arbitrary rebalancing-phase choice - so a switch on that spread has a weaker base to act on). Total 45, unchanged, but the composition changed: the entry is now testable rather than untestable, and the catalogue regains an active non-derivatives member.
- **Conditional funding-extreme reversal-versus-continuation split (the sign of the funding-extreme response conditioned on the direction of the market on the event day). Deferred on 2026-10-20** (seventh cycle) rather than promoted, and recorded here so that it is not rediscovered from scratch. Reason: the published support is one unreviewed single-author preprint whose headline magnitude is far larger than anything else in this literature and therefore needs replication before it is catalogued. Shek (2026, SSRN 7520320) reports 274 funding-rate extreme events across BTC, ETH and SOL from 2021 to 2026, with events on up days reverting by +1.88% over the next 24 hours and events on down days continuing by -1.74%, a +3.62pp gap with a 95% block-bootstrap interval of [2.60, 4.67] and a pooled +6.47pp around seven exogenous shocks. A second source partially corroborates the reversal leg without the condition (Memon, Anklesaria-Dalal and Mishra 2026: extreme *negative* funding on Binance BTC perpetuals predicts a +0.506% 24-hour return, significant under t, Wilcoxon and directional-frequency tests and robust to false-discovery-rate correction, while extreme *positive* funding has no comparable effect). A third source contradicts the negative leg outright and documents cross-asset sign heterogeneity (MarketTrace, 2026: 789 days, three venues, six assets; deeply negative funding predicts nothing on any of the six, with 622 episodes and medians within +/-0.7%, while the top-decile state predicts a negative median 72-hour return on BTC and positive medians on ETH, BNB, XRP and DOGE). The signal is also closely adjacent to the already-rejected CRYPTO-EDGE-002, and the conditional form is a narrowing of a claim whose unconditional form was tested and failed, so it must clear a higher bar before it is promoted, not a lower one. **Reinstatement condition:** an independent preregistered test of the conditional split on a disjoint sample and window, with the event-day conditioning applied at a tradable decision time, and with the positive and negative tails reported separately for each asset. A companion result worth reading alongside it: a second paper by the same author (SSRN 7529458) documents that the *speed* of correction after a funding extreme is dominated by realized volatility (median 27.0 hours at high volatility against 3.0 hours at low), and a third (SSRN 7514718) documents that an unconditional funding-mean-reversion strategy earns Sharpe 1.248 on BTC but -0.084 on ETH and -0.879 on SOL, which is a cross-asset sign instability that any candidate in this family must confront.
- Spot ETF flow pressure (institutional creation demand → temporary spot price pressure with partial reversal). Deferred on 2026-10-20 rather than promoted. Reason: flow endogeneity. Oefele (2025, Economics Letters) finds daily Bitcoin price changes are the primary driver of daily net ETF flows, and "Spot Bitcoin ETF Flows and Bitcoin Returns: Timestamp ..." (SSRN 7545019) reports lagged flows predict returns only marginally in an information-consistent VAR while lagged returns predict flows strongly. Without evidence that a *return-residualized* flow shock has independent price impact in crypto, the idea risks being a repackaged short-term reversal. Data feasibility is already satisfied and free (Farside daily net-flow tables, US spot BTC ETFs from January 2024, ETH from July 2024, URL https://farside.co.uk/btc/). Revisit if a primary source establishes that flow shocks cause returns net of returns in a crypto sample, or if a return-residualized flow signal is specified with an explicit momentum baseline.
- Perp-versus-dated-futures term-structure decomposition (perpetual basis minus nearest quarterly futures basis) as a cleaner proxy for speculative-versus-hedging demand. Deferred on 2026-10-20. Reason: it is a refinement of CRYPTO-EDGE-001 rather than a distinct mechanism, and it adds a roll-matching and data-availability burden (CME quarterly settlements, Binance COIN-M quarterly futures) without an identified comparative gain in evidence quality. Revisit if CRYPTO-EDGE-001's momentum-residualized variant proves weak, since the decomposition is the natural remedy for that confound.
- Stablecoin/venue-flow and on-chain exchange-reserve hypotheses. Deferred: no free, reliably timestamped, survivorship-aware series was confirmed in this cycle, and the mission prioritises low-friction retail-testable inputs. **Updated 2026-10-20 (sixth cycle):** the *stablecoin supply* part of this deferral is resolved — DefiLlama's stablecoin supply history is confirmed free (access 1) — but the stablecoin-supply-growth hypothesis itself has been rejected on endogeneity and contamination grounds, see the rejected list above. The *on-chain exchange-reserve* part remains deferred: free on-chain data exists (mempool.space, blockchain.info, Etherscan, Arkham free tier) but a survivorship-aware, reliably timestamped exchange-reserve series could not be confirmed, and reserve-flow proxies are heavily studied and tend to proxy for price rather than lead it.
- Credit-spread funding-conditions channel (macro / cross-asset, non-derivatives). Deferred on 2026-10-20. Reason: the mechanism and the asymmetry are documented — Lee (2026, European Financial Management) finds that cryptocurrency returns decline when credit spreads widen, particularly during crashes, yet do not recover when spreads narrow, using daily data from January 2021 to February 2025 — and the input is genuinely free (ICE BofA option-adjusted spread series via FRED, no key required). It is not promoted because the published abstract states that the authors required a data provider that treats crypto and equities consistently, which is an obstacle at the stated granularity, and because the effect is a broad risk-off proxy whose incremental content over a Bitcoin-realized-volatility state variable is untested. Revisit if a source establishes an incremental, timing-specific crypto effect of credit-spread changes rather than a contemporaneous co-movement.
- Perp funding as a volatility-forecasting signal (not a return signal). Deferred on 2026-10-20. Reason: the mechanism is documented and the input is
free — a 2026 Journal of Financial Markets paper finds funding-rate-based predictors materially improve forecasts of Bitcoin spot volatility,
remaining significant after controlling for fixed-maturity-futures volatility — but the tradeable expression (long or short variance, or a volatility-
timing overlay) requires a venue or instrument that is not in the retail-free set, and this catalogue has no volatility-instrument entry. Revisit if a
free, retail-accessible expression of a funding-conditioned volatility view is identified; this is a natural companion to the crash-conditionality
restructure of CRYPTO-EDGE-001 and would strengthen its P1 tail test. **Updated 2026-10-20 (seventh cycle):** the identification half of this
deferral is now settled in the deferral's favour. Pindza and Mashele (2026, MDPI Forecasting 8(5):76) give a first-passage framework that forecasts the
probability of an isolated-margin boundary breach conditional on a delayed or smoothed mark and on funding as a persistent collateral drain, on public
price and funding paths, with lower Brier scores for the structural forecast than for historical simulation at 10x over 24-, 72- and 168-hour horizons;
and Shek (2026, SSRN 7529458) shows the speed of correction after a funding extreme is dominated by realized volatility rather than by the funding
level. The deferral therefore now rests on a single point: there is still no free, retail-accessible expression of a funding-conditioned volatility
view, and this catalogue has no volatility-instrument entry to host it.
- **Venue-announced perpetual-contract delisting as a cross-sectional event (contract termination and forced unwinding). Deferred on 2026-10-20** (ninth cycle). Reason: the ninth cycle's data determination makes this family *feasible* on free data for the first time - the Binance archive yields the settlement date of every delisted USD-M perpetual as the last file with a non-zero trade count, and venue announcements are published with dates on the venue's own announcement pages (Binance publishes a delisting notice roughly one to three weeks before settlement, with postponements) - but the family is deferred on an **identification** ground rather than a data ground, and that ground is severe. A venue's product review selects contracts that are weak by the venue's own criteria, so the announcement is a deterministic function of the token's deteriorating fundamentals; any measured underperformance between announcement and settlement is therefore confounded by slow information drift into a token that was already deteriorating, and a matched control on non-delisted but comparably weak tokens is required before any announcement effect can be claimed. A second, milder issue is that the settlement date is a free proxy for the announcement date but the announcement feed itself is not a documented machine-readable free dataset, so the event window would have to be defined as "trailing N days before settlement" with N fixed in advance. **Reinstatement condition:** a preregistered identification design that separates the announcement effect from pre-existing token weakness (a matched control group of weak, non-delisted contracts, and a placebo on announcement dates for contracts that were later relisted), plus confirmation that the delisting announcements can be obtained as a dated list rather than by scraping. Revisit only with that design in place; do not promote on the practitioner observation that tokens fell 5-7% after the November 2024 announcements, which is marketing-adjacent and uncontrolled.
