## This cycle (sixth research cycle)

One hypothesis rejected, two downgraded, one new non-derivatives entry. The cycle's dominant finding is a sign reversal that reorganises the catalogue.

1. **A preregistered external test of the funding-extreme contrarian thesis failed with the wrong sign**, so CRYPTO-EDGE-002 is rejected rather than downgraded. The test (Binance USDT perpetuals, 8 symbols, 17,910 symbol-days, December 2019 to July 2026) used this catalogue's own signal construction — trailing-90-day percentile of daily funding, 10th/90th deciles, fixed before the run — and found 7-day LOW-minus-HIGH of -0.95% with a 95% block-bootstrap interval of [-3.20%, +1.39%] and sign consistency of 4 of 8 symbols against a required 5. Both point-estimate directions leaned the wrong way: HIGH-funding days were followed by higher returns (+3.30% vs +2.35% at 7 days; +12.61% vs +4.83% at 30 days). A momentum-flavored variant was also tested and not promoted, so both signs are closed. This closes the catalogue's largest evidence gap for CRYPTO-EDGE-002, which until now rested only on practitioner anecdote.
2. **The same reversal hits CRYPTO-EDGE-001.** A preregistered test of the perp-basis level on Binance perpetuals (17,899 symbol-days) found the 7-day LOW-minus-HIGH at -2.61% with an interval of [-4.88%, -0.12%] excluding zero *on the wrong side*: high-premium days were followed by +4.20% vs +1.59% at 7 days and +14.8% vs +6.1% at 30 days. This is the closest available test of the entry's directional claim, it covers the post-July-2024 window that no published sample covers, and it contradicts the mean-return form of the prediction. CRYPTO-EDGE-001 is therefore restructured from a directional return claim into a **crash-conditionality** claim, with a mandatory volatility-matched baseline, and is downgraded from 73 to 63.
3. **CRYPTO-EDGE-003 is downgraded from 61 to 58** because its sign is now contested rather than merely uncertain: three independent sources place price discovery on the more heavily traded and typically higher-funding venue, and a preregistered cross-venue test found the high-premium venue was followed by *higher* returns (+3.17% at 7 days, CI [+0.51%, +6.08%], breadth 17/20, surviving a trailing-return trim and a concentration check). The same test found cross-venue dispersion *as a magnitude* to be noise at all three horizons. CRYPTO-EDGE-003's validation plan is reordered so the sign is estimated before any trade expression is specified.
4. **CRYPTO-EDGE-004 added at rank 3, 56/100, confidence low.** The catalogue's first non-derivatives entry: cross-sectional dispersion of crypto returns as a state variable that switches off cross-sectional momentum when the cross-section disagrees most. Published, methodologically explicit prior art (Zhang and Makgolo, April 2026); the blocking constraint is a free point-in-time crypto universe, not the idea.
5. **Standing prior recorded for the next cycle:** in crypto, crowding-style signals (funding extremes, elevated basis, cross-venue premiums) have now been contradicted in three preregistered tests, all in the same direction — they accompany continuation rather than exhaustion. Any new contrarian candidate in this domain must beat that prior before it is promoted.

- CRYPTO-EDGE-001 downgraded 73 -> 63, confidence medium -> low-medium. Robustness 3/5 -> 2/5 and net value 3/5 -> 2/5 on the preregistered perp-basis contradiction, the structural compression of the perp-era basis by the funding mechanism, the documented collapse of the cash-and-carry premium in 2025-2026, and the relocation of perp-funding informational content from direction to volatility. Prediction restructured from mean return to crash conditionality; no other dimension moved.
- CRYPTO-EDGE-003 downgraded 61 -> 58, confidence low-medium -> low. Robustness 3/5 -> 2/5 on the price-discovery challenge to the sign and the noise result for cross-venue dispersion magnitude. Evidence quality and net value held at 2/5 with reasons recorded.
