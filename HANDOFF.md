- **Standing prior to argue against, now instrument-level:** the sign of a carry or crowding signal in crypto is **instrument-dependent**. On dated futures, high carry predicts crashes (peer-reviewed, sample to July 2024). On perpetual funding, carry positively predicts returns (the same authors, 2021 working paper). On retail perps in 2024–2026, both the unconditional contrarian and the unconditional continuation forms fail, and the sign of any effect depends on the direction of the market on the event day and on the asset. **No candidate in this family may be promoted on dated-futures evidence, and every candidate must state its instrument, its asset class and its conditioning state before it can be assessed.**
