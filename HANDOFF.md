## Notes (carry forward)

- **Standing prior to argue against, now instrument-level:** the sign of a carry or crowding signal in crypto is **instrument-dependent**. On dated futures, high carry predicts crashes (peer-reviewed, sample to July 2024). On perpetual funding, carry positively predicts returns (the same authors, 2021 working paper). On retail perps in 2024–2026, both the unconditional contrarian and the unconditional continuation forms fail, and the sign of any effect depends on the direction of the market on the event day and on the asset. **No candidate in this family may be promoted on dated-futures evidence, and every candidate must state its instrument, its asset class and its conditioning state before it can be assessed.**
- **Extended standing prior (ninth cycle):** the crypto crash process is documented to be **first-order and shock-driven** in its two largest recent instances, with the observable footprint in open-interest clearing and price impact rather than in any pre-cascade state variable. Any new candidate in this family must answer: what in the state variable is not already a proxy for the common factor, and does the candidate survive on the observable footprint rather than on the precursor.
- **Standing prior (tenth cycle) — select versus harvest:** a cross-sectional book that **ranks and selects** is structurally more exposed to universe bias than one that **harvests** every paying stream, because a hindsight list hands a selection its winners while for a harvest it barely changes which streams are averaged. The one measurement in this catalogue of that exposure (the momentum base effect retaining 42% of its Sharpe on a point-in-time universe) is of the selection type, and the preregistered tier reports the same distinction with a correct out-of-sample prediction (carry retains 86% of Sharpe, momentum 58%). It is recorded as a hypothesis, not a rule, because its own author records it as one. **Eleventh-cycle extension — the prior has a cost leg as well as a universe-bias leg:** front-end traders are 96.0% of forced-liquidation volume and bear $344m of forced losses against $118m returned by auto-deleveraging, so a selection book sits on the side that absorbs the forced unwind, in a market whose depth is 66-79% of pre-event and still contracting when it must exit. **Fourteenth-cycle extension — the prior now has a cross-sectional leg as well:** on 2024-08-05 the small-cap COIN-M perps' open interest collapsed far more than the majors' (-40.8% for RUNEUSD_PERP against -13.6% for BTCUSD_PERP) and their forced-flow share of opening open interest was two orders of magnitude larger (37.94% for ENSUSD_PERP against 1.96% for BTCUSD_PERP), so a selection book forced to exit in the small caps is exiting where the market is thinnest and where the price impact is largest.
